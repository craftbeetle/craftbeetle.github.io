Second Year Review

26.08.02 BACK TO BLOGS

It's been two whole years since I started Craft Beetle and it's still going strong. Craft Beetle is now a solid part of my life. I've kept updating it every sunday and I have had the great pleasure of having all of my closest friends join me in making websites as well as friends of friends and comrades at large. Our saturday night website review session is now no longer a segment of the night but the highlight of the week. We each put in the effort to write, draw, photograph, collect, create and otherwise add to our website and we discuss them all together.

Weekly website review has been a great motivator for each of us to do our best to not only create but do it consistently. Some who where the most resistant to the idea at first have become the best of us, making creative pages and collecting niche curiosities for our collective. I am so glad that the root has set. Many of these friends have entirely deleted their social media and other account and solely focus on their own websites.

As for myself, I feel that Craft Beetle has had a solid year of consistent work. More so in content addition rather than full reinvention, though some pages have been changed and updated to match what I have learned. I am still trying to figure out how to make the wiki work on a creative and design level. I also am not blind to my neglect of the game dev and craft pages, I've simply been very busy in life.

Here's to another year of Craft Beetle and many more, inshaAllah!

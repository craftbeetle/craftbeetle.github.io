HEADER AND FOOTER UPDATING

I keep an up to date changelog and upon fan request I created a script that converts that log to RSS. It doesn't put the actual contents on RSS but it will pop up on your feed as a weekly reminder to come check out my website.

import xml.etree.ElementTree as ET
from xml.dom import minidom

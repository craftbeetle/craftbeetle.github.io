Revolutionary Club

25.11.30 BACK TO BLOGS

All high-schools, colleges, and universities should have revolutionary student clubs. These clubs should be equal partners with the teachers/professor/staff unions and work together to support strikes, make demands, and raise class consciousness. Make your club official if you are allowed, and if you are not allowed then make it unofficial but still do all the same activities.

Conduct yearly surveys of your fellow students, your teachers, and staff regarding learning and working conditions. Publish your polls for the world to see, compare, and discuss.

Read revolutionary, anti-imperial, anti-capitalist material. Organize and hold education events for your fellow students and staff. Make propaganda, memes, zines. Take over the school news paper or run your own competing news paper.

Influence the curriculum towards truth. Pre-read the history, civics, and English coursework and prepare critique. Learn the real inventors, scientists, and mathematicians who built the math and sciences and educate your fellow students.

You will draw push-back, suppression, and clamp downs. Good. That means it's working. All aspects of life must be organized. Today's student communist is tomorrow's siege breaker, pipeline buster, world revolution builder.

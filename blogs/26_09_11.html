Obsidian Blues

26.09.11 BACK TO BLOGS

I've been using Obsidian MD daily for over 3 years. It has been going mostly well, though I have definitely spent too much "doing Obsidian" and not enough time doing what I'm actually wanting to do. I've fallen into an over organized and unproductive trap. Finally, something happened in the mobile app development that caused it to load really slowly and that has caused me to use it less and less.

It's time to retire Obsidian I think. Time to go back to basics and just get stuff done. It's okay if I don't know exactly when I did tasks in the past and all other other minutia that I tracked and never did anything with.

I'm starting to take regular pen and paper notes and going back to a regular calendar and other types of apps for the rest. Some (very positive) life changes also make it more important for me to be able to collaborate on domestic matter and to be on my phone/PC less. I'm hopeful that this will have a positive impact.

Fundamentally, the system itself won't save an unproductive person. But a system can hinder a productive person. I need to shed the weight.
